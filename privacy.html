Privacy policy

Cook my DutyLeave · Updated 13 October 2026

This extension creates duty-leave PDFs for Sahrdaya students. It is an independent helper and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the college or Etlab.

Information used

When you open or refresh the extension on Etlab, it reads the displayed attendance month, semester, student name, absent dates, periods and subjects. It fetches your profile using your existing Etlab login and extracts only your name and SR number. Other profile fields are not extracted or stored. Your program/event, class roll number, branch, division and any corrections are used to fill your PDF.

Storage and sharing

PDFs are generated on your device. Drafts and up to eight previews are held in Chrome session storage, which is cleared when Chrome restarts or the extension is reloaded, disabled or uninstalled. Previews expire after 24 hours and older previews may be removed when new ones are created. Forget draft removes saved form input and selections; it does not delete previews or downloaded PDFs. Downloaded files remain wherever you save them.

No data is sent to the developer, analytics services, advertisers or other third parties. Only read-only HTTPS requests to Sahrdaya Etlab are made. We do not collect passwords, sell user data, track browsing outside Etlab, submit applications or modify your profile. The extension's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • Etlab site access and scripting: read attendance and verify profile identity.
  • Storage: keep session drafts and PDF previews locally.
  • Downloads: save the PDF when you request it.

Your choices and contact

You can correct autofilled name and SR number, clear your draft, close previews, remove downloaded files, or uninstall the extension. PDFs contain personal information; share them only with intended recipients.

Questions: project issue tracker. Avoid posting personal student information in public issues.
